## Qualitative Interview Questions

For 10-15 deep-dive interviews with interested respondents:

1. Walk me through how you currently shop for your kids' clothes
2. What's the most you've ever spent on a single item for your child?
3. Tell me about a time you were disappointed with kids' clothing purchase
4. If money were no object, what brands would you dress your kids in?
5. How do you feel about second-hand/vintage for kids?
6. What would your perfect kids' wardrobe solution look like?
7. How do you decide what to keep when your child grows out of something?
8. What do you think about the environmental impact of kids' clothing?
9. How much time do you spend shopping for kids vs yourself?
10. What would make you recommend a kids' clothing service to a friend?
