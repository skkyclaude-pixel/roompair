# May 12, 2026 - Business Research Day

## Companies Analyzed

### OR Collective (UK)
- Kids clothing rental subscription
- Monthly fee for unlimited swaps
- Partners with Bobo Choses, Tiny Cottons, Liewood, etc.
- Sustainability-focused, circular economy
- Waitlist-based membership

### Totter and Tumble (UK/US)
- Stylish play mats that look like decor
- $139-$359 price range
- Reversible, memory foam, easy clean, certified safe
- William Morris collaborations
- Key message: "Play mats that don't look like play mats"

### Binibamba (UK)
- Premium sheepskin products
- Merino sheepskin, British Made
- Pram liners, blankets, booties, snuggle vests
- Eco-tanned, hypoallergenic
- Awards, Vogue recommended
- Price point: £100-300+

## Irish Market Context

- Ireland has highest birth rate in Europe
- Strong online shopping culture (similar to UK)
- Currency: EUR
- Competitors: Small independent retailers, some UK shops shipping to Ireland
- Gap: No dedicated Irish aspirational children's brand

## Identified Gaps in Market

1. **Play mats in Ireland** - Totter & Tumble doesn't ship widely to Ireland, premium play mats underserved
2. **Scandinavian import** - Many Danish/Swedish brands (Liewood, Kiddo, Moulin-Ry) not well represented
3. **Rental/clothing rotation** - OR Collective not in Irish market
4. **Sustainable baby goods** - Gap for Irish-sourced/European sustainable baby products

## European/Asian Products Not in UK

- **Danish brands**: Dlhs, Highty, Kid5, Bags on Board
- **Swedish**: Little Jellycat (though UK has some)
- **Dutch**: B shuttle, Janod
- **Japanese**: Many wooden toys (Tokyo Story, Totem)
- **Korean**: Eco-friendly baby brands (Burt's Bees but Korean origin)

## Business Idea Opportunities

1. **Irish Play Mat Brand** - Design-forward play mats for Irish market (Totter & Tumble gap)
2. **European Children's Goods Curator** - Import Scandinavian brands to UK/Ireland
3. **Kids Clothing Rental Ireland** - Bring subscription model to Ireland
4. **Premium Irish Baby Goods** - Sheepskin/merino, locally made or sourced
