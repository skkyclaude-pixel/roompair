## 🎯 The Problem

- **Screen time is the default** — Parents want alternatives, but kids reject anything "boring"
- **Yoto/Tonies are one-way** — Kids listen, but don't participate
- **Voice recording is niche** — Yoto has it as a side feature, not a core differentiator
- **No interactive audio stories** — Voice-first choose-your-own-adventure doesn't exist for kids

---

## 💡 The Solution

A screen-free audio player that combines:

1. **Anyone Can Read** — Parents, grandparents, relatives record their voice. Kids choose who reads.
2. **Interactive Adventures** — Kids talk to the story, shape what happens next.

**The combo no one else offers:** Emotional personalization + active imagination
