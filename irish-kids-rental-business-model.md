# Irish Kids Clothing Rental Business Model
## Premium European Brands | Circular Fashion for Kids

---

## Executive Summary

**The Concept:** A kids clothing rental subscription service for Ireland, offering premium European (primarily Scandinavian) children's brands. Parents pay a monthly fee to access a rotating wardrobe of high-quality, stylish kids' clothes - sustainable, convenient, and aspirational.

**The Gap:** OR Collective (UK) doesn't serve Ireland properly. One small Irish competitor exists (see below), but market is underserved.

**Existing Competition:**
- **My Little Wardrobe** (@mylittlewardrobe.ie) - Instagram-based, ~1,400 followers
- Small, not fully scaled
- Could be acquisition target or shows market validation

**Investment Required:** €150,000-250,000
**Time to Launch:** 6-9 months
**Target Year 1:** 500 subscribers
**Year 3 Potential:** 3,000+ subscribers, €2M+ revenue

---

## The Business Model Canvas

### 1. Value Proposition

**For Parents:**
- "A full wardrobe for your little ones, on rotation"
- Premium European brands (Scandinavian-focused)
- No stains/wear charges - we take care of everything
- Sustainable choice - circular fashion
- Flexibility - swap sizes as kids grow
- Convenient - delivered to door

**For Brands:**
- Additional revenue stream for excess inventory
- Exposure to new customers
- Sustainability credentials enhanced

### 2. Target Customer

**Primary:**
- Location: Dublin (60%), Cork, Galway, Limerick (40%)
- Age: 28-42 years old
- Household income: €60,000+
- Kids age: 6 months - 7 years
- Values: Sustainability, design, quality over price
- Shopping: Online, Instagram, quality-conscious

**Secondary:**
- Gift buyers (grandparents, aunties)
- Multiple children (twins = big market)

### 3. Revenue Model

**Subscription Tiers:**

| Tier | Pieces | Monthly Fee (EUR) | Target |
|------|--------|-------------------|--------|
| Starter | 5 | €35 | 1 child,试用 |
| Standard | 10 | €55 | 1 child, regular rotation |
| Plus | 15 | €75 | 1 child + frequent changes |
| Family | 20 | €95 | 2+ children, twins |

**Additional Revenue Streams:**
- **Late fees** - €5/day after 14 days (encourages rotation)
- **Stain damage** - €10-25 per item (if unrepairable)
- **Extended keep** - €5/item to keep permanently
- **Gift subscriptions** - Prepaid 3/6/12 months
- **Brand partnerships** - Co-marketing with brands

**Price Comparison:**
- OR Collective UK: £24-85/month
- Target Ireland: €35-95/month (competitive with UK pricing + EUR)

### 5. Unit Economics

**Per Subscriber Economics:**
- Average subscription: €55/month
- Average customer lifespan: 18 months
- Items per rotation: 3-4 per month
- Clean cycles per item: 8-10x per life

**LTV Calculation:**
```
Monthly fee: €55
Gross margin (60%): €33
Churn rate: 5.5%/month
LTV = €33 / 0.055 = €600
```

**CAC Targets:**
- Paid social: €30-50 per subscriber
- Word of mouth: €10 per referral
- Influencer: €100-300 per post

**Inventory Mix:**
- Baby (6-24 months): 30%
- Toddler (2-4 years): 40%
- Kids (4-7 years): 30%

**Condition Tiers:**
- New with tags: 20%
- Excellent: 50%
- Good: 25%
- Fair (clearance): 5%

### 2. Cleaning & Returns

**The Cleaning Problem:**
- Need professional partner (like OR Collective's ACS)
- Options:
  1. Partner with Irish laundry (local)
  2. Third-party medical-grade cleaning
  3. In-house with commercial machines

**Recommended:** Partner with established Irish laundry service initially, then in-house as scale.

**Return Flow:**
1. Customer requests swap in app
2. Pre-paid return label generated
3. Item returned in compostable satchel
4. Inspected, cleaned, re-added to inventory
5. New items shipped within 48 hours

### 3. Logistics

**Shipping Strategy:**
- Inbound (to customer): €5-8 per delivery (An Post, Fastway)
- Outbound (returns): €3-5 (pre-paid label)
- Free shipping above €55 subscription

**Warehouse:**
- Start: 500 sq ft, Dublin area
- Scale: 2,000 sq ft by Year 3

### 4. Technology

**Platform Requirements:**
- Web+App: Shopify + custom rental app
- Subscription management: Recharge or Loop
- Inventory tracking: Custom
- Size management: User profiles, growth tracking

**Tech Stack (MVP):**
- Platform: Shopify Plus (€2,000/m)
- App: Flutter (€15,000 dev)
- Subscriptions: Recharge (€500/m)
- Email: Klaviyo (€200/m)
